# 我的大学时间轴

从技术积累到实习、暑期实习和秋招，记录大学四年的重要规划。

      </article>
    </div>
  </div>

    <div class="timeline-cards">
      <article class="timeline-card">
        <h2>投递日常实习</h2>
        <p>推荐在三、四月份开始投递日常实习，争取获得实习机会。</p>
      </article>
    </div>
  </div>

  <div class="timeline-group">
    <div class="timeline-dates">
      <div class="timeline-date"><b>2024.07</b><span>大二暑假</span></div>
    </div>
    <div class="timeline-cards">
      <article class="timeline-card">
        <h2>把握日常实习机会</h2>
        <p>八月底、九月初一定要把握机会投递日常实习。</p>
        <div class="timeline-note">这个时间段的 HC（招聘名额）可能较多，要把握机会。</div>
      </article>
    </div>
  </div>

  <div class="timeline-group">
    <div class="timeline-dates">
      <div class="timeline-date"><b>2024.09</b><span>大三上</span></div>
    </div>
    <div class="timeline-cards">
      <article class="timeline-card">
        <h2>规划实习经历</h2>
        <p>卷实习，每段实习可以按三个月规划。核心部门可以考虑多待一段时间。</p>
        <p>日常实习较难接触核心部门，因此最好在暑期实习前卷出两段实习经历。</p>
      </article>
    </div>
  </div>

  <div class="timeline-group">
    <div class="timeline-dates">
      <div class="timeline-date"><b>2025.01</b><span>大三寒假</span></div>
    </div>
    <div class="timeline-cards">
      <article class="timeline-card">
        <h2>为暑期实习做准备</h2>
        <p>提前准备简历与面试，为接下来的暑期实习招聘做准备。</p>
      </article>
    </div>
  </div>

  <div class="timeline-group">
    <div class="timeline-dates">
      <div class="timeline-date"><b>2025.02</b><span>大三下</span></div>
    </div>
    <div class="timeline-cards">
      <article class="timeline-card">
        <h2>投递暑期实习</h2>
        <p>二月底、三月初，各厂会陆续开启暑期实习招聘，要尽早关注信息并投递。</p>
      </article>
    </div>
  </div>

  <div class="timeline-group">
    <div class="timeline-dates">
      <div class="timeline-date"><b>2025.07</b><span>大三暑假</span></div>
    </div>
    <div class="timeline-cards">
      <article class="timeline-card">
        <h2>准备秋招</h2>
        <p>七月底、八月初，各厂会陆续开启秋招面试和招聘流程，要尽早投递。</p>
        <div class="timeline-note">举例：美团2025 届的秋招在 2024 年 7 月 29 日开启。具体时间以当年公告为准。</div>
      </article>
    </div>
  </div>

  <div class="timeline-group">
    <div class="timeline-dates">
      <div class="timeline-date"><b>2025.09</b><span>大四上</span></div>
    </div>
    <div class="timeline-cards">
      <article class="timeline-card">
        <h2>关注剩余秋招机会</h2>
        <p>十二月份，部分公司仍可能有秋招机会，可以继续关注招聘信息。</p>
        <p>如果已经拿到 offer，可以根据自身情况考虑 gap 一年；如果还没有拿到 offer，可以继续关注春招或考虑考研。</p>
      </article>
    </div>
  </div>

  <div class="timeline-group">

      </article>
    </div>
  </div>
